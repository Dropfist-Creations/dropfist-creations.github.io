Built for the table, framed for the hand.

Resolve combines large readable play surfaces with profile tools, match history, keyword lookup, and shareable setup flows.

Profiles

Keep track of it all.

Create your own player profile, manage your commanders, and track each deck's win/loss record.

Commander Setup

Pick your own art.

Search commander by name and set the artwork you want so each profile feels personal at the table.

Shared Players

Import your pod.

Add or import the rest of your playgroups profiles to keep track of everyone's win/loss records.

Sharing

Share profiles.

Share or import profiles to keep your stats with you across multiple devices and playgroups.

Duel Layout

Quick access to menus.

Tap the central logo to open your Profile, Game Settings, Codex Lookup or Reset the game.

Clean Counters

Random colours for guests.

Use a stripped-back counter layout for quick games, prototypes, or tables that prefer clarity.

Table View

Life totals everyone can read.

Four-player commander layout with large totals, player artwork, and fast table-side controls.

Match Results

Save wins before they vanish.

Capture the winner, commander, and eliminated players so profile records stay accurate over time.

Game Settings

Shape the table before turn one.

Set player count, portrait or landscape layouts, starting life totals, commander damage and much more.

Match Codex

Review the game state.

See match time, starting player, winner status, and each player's key stats mid-session.

History

Understand how the table moved.

Review life changes, timing, and sources. Made a mistake? Revert back to any point instantly.

Keywords

Look up rules at table speed.

Search common MTG keywords instantly, without breaking the flow of a game.
