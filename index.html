Resolve

MTG life counter

Welcome to Resolve: your tabletop companion for Magic: The Gathering.

Designed with simplicity, speed, and readability in mind, Resolve helps you track life totals, commander damage, and more without getting in the way of the game.

Create custom player profiles, assign your favourite commanders, and automatically track your win/loss record over time. Share and import profiles with your POD to discover who really has the strongest Commander deck, once and for all!

Whether you're playing a quick duel or settling in for a four-player Commander marathon, Resolve keeps everything organised so you can focus on what really matters: great games with great people.

No ads. No subscriptions. No nonsense.

Just a fast, reliable life counter built by a Magic player who wanted something better.
